Common Inquiries

Clear answers for sophisticated investors looking to expand their Dubai footprint.

Is there any brokerage fee?
expand_more

For primary market (off-plan) properties directly from developers, our consultation and transaction management services are provided at zero brokerage cost to the investor. We are compensated by the development partners, ensuring you receive expert advice without overhead.

Can overseas investors purchase?
expand_more

Absolutely. Dubai is one of the most accessible markets for international capital. You can complete the entire reservation and transaction process remotely. We utilize digital signing and secure payment gateways to facilitate cross-border investments seamlessly.

What are the typical ROI expectations?
expand_more

Dubai consistently offers some of the world's highest rental yields, typically ranging from 6% to 9% net. Additionally, prime areas have seen capital appreciation of 15-20% annually in high-demand cycles. Our 'Investment Tree' reports provide specific projections for every listing.

Is my investment protected by law?
expand_more

Yes. All off-plan investments are protected by Escrow laws mandated by the Dubai Land Department (DLD). Payments are made into project-specific escrow accounts and released only as construction milestones are verified by RERA-approved auditors.
